How to read this

Lineup gain is the difference between your best legal lineup after the add/drop and your best lineup if you do nothing.

Injury cover repeats that comparison with one named starter unavailable. It is not added to the normal projection.

FAAB guidance is a spending policy, not the amount guaranteed to win. No paid bid is recommended in this sample.

What happened to “Waiver Edge”?

The new design favors plain labels: lineup gain, contingency benefit, and future cost. One opaque score should not combine different kinds of value.
